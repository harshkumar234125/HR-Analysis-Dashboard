 HR-Analysis-Dashboard

 
📈 About this project


Understanding employee demographics, salary distribution, and workforce attrition is essential for effective HR planning. This project provides an interactive view of workforce patterns, helping stakeholders explore employee composition and identify areas that may need attention.

✅ Approach 

Developed a two-page Power BI dashboard covering Workforce Analytics and Attrition Insights. The first page explores employee headcount, gender distribution, salary by job title, and workforce demographics. The second page examines employee growth over time, department-wise exits, salary comparisons across departments and age groups, and workforce distribution by business unit. Used Power Query for data preparation, DAX for analytical measures, and interactive slicers for exploring workforce data across different dimensions.

📊 Key Insights

• Analyzed a workforce of 1,000 employees, including 518 female and 482 male employees.

• Identified 915 active employees and 85 employees who left, providing an overview of workforce retention.

• Compared employee exits across departments, with Engineering recording 17 exits, followed by IT with 16 and Marketing with 15.

• Examined salary differences across departments and age groups to understand workforce compensation patterns.

• Explored employee demographics by age, ethnicity, country, and business unit to support more informed HR analysis.
